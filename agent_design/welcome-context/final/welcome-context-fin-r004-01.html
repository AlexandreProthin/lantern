Motion language

The closest to r003-03: same choreography, same speed class, but the motion decelerates into place instead of passing it and springing back. Fast start, long soft stop (cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 380 ms); the button slides out from behind the edge in 240 ms on focus and back in 170 ms when you leave.

Try it

Arrival focuses the field, so the button appears at once. Leave the field (click a tone, the page) and the button leaves; come back and it returns. Shift+Enter opens the direction and relocates the button; Backspace in an empty direction or Esc closes it and the button travels back. Use Slow ×4 and the Play buttons in the prototype bar to watch the choreography.

Frozen, and new from the brief

Behaviour is timer-driven, never waiting on animation frames (the Welcome recipe rule). Everything visual is r002-02 frozen and the choreography is r003-03's, without its bounce; only easing and duration differ between the three (v0.1.0 base, the capsule, the two-part card). New from the brief: Carry the lantern in exists only while the subject or direction has the cursor (spec change to WG-1/WG-3, to be written).
