Interaction hypothesis

The lightest possible chrome: Welcome is pixel-identical until the user presses Shift+Enter. The field becomes a two-part writing surface with a hairline between the subject and its direction, and the counters say which part is which. A hint under the field is the only discovery path, so it is the main risk of this one. A pasted paragraph is split at the first line break, which makes a long first line a hard case.
