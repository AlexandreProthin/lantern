# Ground rules

The rules of [v0.1.0](/plans/v0-1-0.md) and [v0.2.0](/plans/v0-2-0.md) still hold: every task cites its
spec ids; a spec that turns out wrong is changed first, then the code, after asking the user; the frame
knows no room (only `app/src/rooms/<id>/`, the room list and tests may name `learn` or `choose`); strings
live in `app/src/ui/strings.ts` (PQ-4).

Amber is frame code (`app/src/frame/amber/`): it names no room. What differs per room (tips, guide lines,
bottom resting positions) comes from the room definition's `companion` member (RS-3, CO-23); the halo and
the light on the frame take room tokens, the flame and body never do (CO-55).

**Branch and order.** Work happens on `claude/lantern-amber`, one commit per slice. The new top bar
(`agent_design/top-bar/RECIPE.md`, r005-02) is being built **in the same version, in its own worktree**
(`claude/lantern-top-bar`; the user, 2026-10-17). Slices 1 to 4 do not touch the header and run first;
slice 5 hangs Amber from the bar and puts *Ask* in the capsule, so it starts once the top bar branch is
merged into this one (on the user's word). If the bar is late, slices 5 to 8 can be built against a
header stub with the bar's geometry (56 px, the capsule's right edge) and rebased; this is the fallback,
not the plan.

Checks run for every slice: `npm run check` 0/0, the TypeScript unit tests, the Rust tests, and the
Playwright suite (fake provider) on a private port. Amber's prompt also needs the three-run eval gate on
Haiku 5.5 (PQ-33, CO-48). Push only when the user asks.

# Slice 6. Moving Amber

- Grab and drag; the rope extends and retracts within the top quarter of the window and the release
  swings (CO-12); resting positions hinted while dragged; flying (*heat and lift*) or falling (*descent
  and landing*) to the nearest one (CO-14); *a few steps* in at a bottom spot (CO-50). The spot per
  space is saved (CO-15).
- The rope as a small verlet chain (fixed step, a few segments), drawn as a curve, never a straight
  static line; the swing is the chain plus Amber's mass settling.
- Reduced motion: no swing, flight or fall; a move goes straight to the spot (CO-56).
- **Exit:** unit tests for snapping, nearest resting position and the chain's settling; Playwright: drag
  to a bottom spot in each space, restart, the spot is kept; reduced motion moves straight.
- **Done 2026-10-17**: `place.ts` gains resting spots (`RestSpec` on the room's `companion.rests`, RS-3: a corner of
  an element of the space, a little inside; none found, the space's two bottom corners), the drop rule and spot ids
  that keep a rope length (`hang-3:42`); `rope.ts` is the Verlet chain (8 segments, 120 Hz, the end heavier than the
  rope; held with 6 % slack so it bows) and the flight and fall paths; `Amber.svelte` grabs after 5 px, hints every
  spot while held (the target lit), keeps the rope within the top quarter, and on release swings, flies or falls.
  Rests are measured from the page and again when the space's parts appear or resize. Checks: `npm run check` 0/0,
  174 TS tests, Playwright 77 passed, 2 skipped (one flaky reload in the new fall test fixed by waiting for the
  debounced save, as the other tests do). In the preview the drop and the landing looked right; the swing could
  not be watched there (the pane was hidden, which pauses animation frames) and is covered by Playwright.
  **Points decided in code, to confirm:**
  1. Choose's Survey gets Study's three rests (same graph and panel frame); CO-13 names only Study.
  2. Each rest sits just above the controls in its corner: 132 px up at the graph's left (legend, *Open*), 72 px
     at its right (zoom), 140 px in the panel (composer).
  3. Settled, the rope hangs straight (a weighted rope does); it bows and moves only while Amber moves.
  4. A hanging spot keeps the rope length it was dropped at (0 to 160 px); *Move to…* uses the default 14 px.
  5. A throw is capped to a gentle push; a swing settles within 6 s at most.
  6. Esc while holding Amber puts it back where it was.
  7. Flights arc, falls accelerate; Amber's pose during them is a still pose until slice 7's gestures.
  8. The bubble and the menu open above a resting Amber, under a hanging one.

# Risks to retire early

| Risk | Where it is retired |
|---|---|
| Haiku 5.5 names the wrong category or breaks the tag | Slice 2's evals, before any character work; the gate and the logged fallback |
| The CLI's start-up makes Ask Amber feel slow | Slice 2 measures time to first word with the real CLI; the thinking motion covers it, the [local models](/plans/backlog.md) experiment comes after |

| The top bar is late or changes its geometry | Slices 1 to 4 need no bar; the stub fallback above |
| The new motions feel off | Slice 7 is judged by the user in the app; an identity round if needed |
| Animation cost on a busy window | Nothing runs when hidden or still; the rope steps only while moving; the idle perf tests stay green |
| Room words leak into the frame | The review question *does this file name `learn` or `choose`?* on every slice |
