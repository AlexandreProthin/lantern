Use case: logo-brand. Asset: ONE landscape concept contact sheet for eight distinct proposals for Lantern's visual identity and future companion. Exactly EIGHT illustrations total, each a SINGLE character study, arranged in a spacious uniform FOUR-column TWO-row grid, left to right top row 01 02 03 04, bottom row 05 06 07 08. No extra pose studies, no thumbnails, no scene, no packaging. Use landscape 3:2 sheet proportions and fill the available sheet cleanly while keeping each entire design uncropped and separated. Small plain legible number under each concept, exact text only: "01", "02", "03", "04", "05", "06", "07", "08". No other text.

Shared brief: a welcoming intelligent object-guide or light entity, helpful reassuring slightly mischievous warm presence for serious curious open-minded adults. It should feel useful and welcome wherever it brings light. Expressive and playful in potential, calmly posed here. The lantern is animated by its almost-living CONTAINED CENTRAL FLAME, not an empty chamber and not a generic external cartoon face. Morphology must be clearly different between the eight. Show structural volume with minimal flat value planes; editorial rough design studies, comparable medium-low finish. Palette shared across ALL concepts: charcoal, midgray and ivory-white only, plain light gray/off-white backdrop. Do not use color to distinguish proposals. Light comes from clear bright inner flame against structural frame, restrained soft halo only; no decorative sparkles. Every internal flame must be large enough to be the focal presence. Paper variants can be pale translucent with a contrasting interior core. Friendly proportions without chibi infant proportions.

Avoid: big cartoon eyes, cute feet, little shoes, gloved hands, character limbs, baby faces, grinning faces on housings, handbags, speakers, TV/robot screens, generic camping/kerosene lantern pictograms, Christmas lanterns, ornate metal lamps, tassels, realistic insect legs, blue sci-fi lighting, hyperreal materials, excessive small details. No uncontrolled dancing flames; expressions conveyed by a contained leaning/curling core and subtle posture. Explore lantern/paper-lantern structure, not the previously rejected abstract pebble/leaf/ribbon territories.

Numbered designs, mandatory placement and distinct structures:
01. A compact geometric lantern with a clearly square luminous inner chamber, thick beveled charcoal corner frame and a LOW triangular carry loop distinct from a handbag arch. One sizeable living white flame inside, leaning inquisitively; internal flame is the focal presence, no face painted on the outside. Stable base, no feet.
02. A squat HEXAGONAL lantern housing with angular integrated small handle and a generous luminous interior; the charcoal housing is visibly six-sided, not square or circular. A bold gently hooked white flame fills the center as the living presence. Slight attentive tilt. No feet, no external eyes.
03. An OPEN architectural lantern: low solid base, just two thick slanted upright protective rails and a short angular top bridge/handle. A large substantial living white flame is fully visible between the rails. Slender upright proportions, no rectangular enclosure, no glass box, no camping kerosene apparatus. The flame subtly curls toward the viewer.
04. A ROUND PAPER LANTERN companion with a compact ribbed folded sphere, flat small top and bottom caps, a tiny angular hanging loop. Four or five broad structural folds maximum, translucent pale body and a clearly visible living charcoal/white flame core. No face outside, no feet, no red festival decoration, no tassel. Tilt the entire body gently for attention.
05. A FACETED PAPER LANTERN, diamond-octagonal folded envelope with two broad crisp side planes, small open top loop and a softly alive flame inside. This is a portable welcoming light entity, not an origami bird or gem. Neutral pale folded shell, charcoal edge structure, no tassels or face. Wide compact faceted silhouette, distinguish from the round paper lantern.
06. A LOW WIDE oblong paper-lantern companion, cushion-shaped ribbed enclosure with thick side end-caps, no handle arch, small top hook only. Gentle resting posture without legs.
07. A lantern-firefly bridge: compact pear-shaped translucent luminous abdomen framed by two charcoal protective side arcs, small angular carry loop functioning like two short joined antennae. One living white flame clearly contained within the body. The lantern structure must dominate, insect lineage only in a gently poised organic tilt. No wings, no segmented legs, no baby face.
08. A living flame suspended inside a SINGLE thick polygonal teardrop protective lantern cage, with a short top carry stem and open bottom cradle. Housing and internal flame clearly separate; a buoyant slightly off-center flame with two tiny subtle negative-space eye slits, no smiling mouth. Frame rests in a calm floating pose. No limbs, no bug anatomy, no generic glowing blob without a lantern cage.
