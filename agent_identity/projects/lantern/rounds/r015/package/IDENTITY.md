## Invariants and responsive variants
Keep suspension loop, split frame, asymmetric flame and rounded base recognizable. Keep lower flame bowl and eyes anchored within the actor. Feet are optional; suspension attaches to loop. Do not stretch proportions or recolor individual layers arbitrarily.
Favicon: neutral monochrome, no eyes/feet/glow/motion; 16 uses optical enlarged-loop source, 24/32/48 master. Light ink for dark backgrounds, dark ink for light backgrounds. 16 remains delicate under blur; native DPI/perceptual recognition not universally proven.
Mark: face-free warm layered fire with static neutral contour, supplied 64/128/256/512. This is a derived system proposal from accepted style, not separately accepted geometry. Companion: accepted face/feet vocabulary, six static poses at 64/128/256 and editable animated runtime.
Minimum delivered colored size 64px is a working export choice, not proven production minimum. Prefer monochrome favicon below 64 until native perception establishes a smaller colored variant.
Clear space proposal: preserve full source viewBox, including glow/feet/attachment room. Raster PNGs use transparent square contain fit; do not trim halo merely to align apparent ink. No independently approved clear-space metric yet.

## Motion
Outer 1200ms contour cycle, inner 2400ms phase-offset curves; lower bowl anchored, face/frame steady. Runtime uses six finite accepted gesture timings and playback controls. Default still; pause freezes; stop stabilizes over 180ms; hide/background cancels; show stays still. OS reduced preference activates static fallbacks. Do not infer automatic idle loops or product-event triggers from these preview scenes. Neutral icon never requires motion.

## Background and limits
Dark sample #181818 and light diagnostic background are tests, not a product theme decision. Light-background halo naturally weaker; do not boost glow without renewed concentration/contrast review. Product Learn/other room accents, distraction in real use, browser DPI, arbitrary gesture chaining, profile/back motion and permanent idle policy remain unresolved. Gait has no contact physics; lift heat cue/descent gravity are stylized. Native tests generated; sampled visual QA does not prove every size.
